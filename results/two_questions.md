| cohort | population | discrimination AUC | 95% CI | time-gradient rho |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GSE79362 | South African adolescent LTBI | 0.791 (38/106) | [0.70, 0.87] | +0.417 (n=33) |

The signature discriminates progressors in all three cohorts, including GSE94438 (AUC 0.68, CI above 0.5). The result reported as a failure in earlier sections is specifically the time gradient, which is present in GSE79362 and absent in GSE94438.

GSE107994 has no continuous time-to-diagnosis field, so the gradient cannot be computed there; its role is to confirm that discrimination holds in a third, independent population.
